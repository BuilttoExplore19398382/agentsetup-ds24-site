Frequently asked questions

Is this a subscription?

No. Both products are one-time purchases. There are no recurring charges.

How is the work delivered?

Everything is delivered remotely over the internet. Agent Setup Starter is built and deployed into your online tools, and the code is handed over digitally. Hourly Consulting takes place on a video call. There is no physical product.

What counts as one workflow and two integrations?

A workflow is one repeatable process, such as routing new inquiries, processing incoming documents, or updating a CRM from email. An integration is one outside tool the workflow connects to, such as your email, CRM, spreadsheet, or chat app. We confirm the exact scope with you in writing before building.

Do I really own the code?

Yes. Once your purchase is complete, the custom code we write for your Starter build is yours. Third-party tools it connects to remain subject to their own terms and fees.

What do I need to provide?

Access to the tools the workflow connects to (or a test account), and someone available to answer questions during scoping and testing.

Who will I work with?

Cooper Hathaway, who operates Agent Setup and runs every engagement.

How do I get help or ask a question before buying?

Email cooper@agentsetup.io or visit our Contact Us page.
