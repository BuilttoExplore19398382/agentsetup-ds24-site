Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Agent Setup ("we", "us"), operated by Cooper Hathaway as a sole proprietor in California, United States, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit this website or buy one of our services.

Information we collect

  • Order information. When you buy, our online reseller Digistore24 collects your name, email address, billing address, and payment details. Digistore24 shares your name, email address, and order details with us so we can deliver your service. We never receive your full card number.
  • Information you send us. Messages, files, and details you provide by email or during scoping and consulting sessions.
  • Access you grant. For Agent Setup Starter builds, the tool access or test accounts you choose to give us, used only to build and test your automation.
  • Technical data. This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Our web host may record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the service you bought, schedule sessions, and communicate about your order.
  • To process refunds and handle support requests.
  • To send occasional updates or newsletters, only if you have agreed to receive them.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Newsletters: how to opt out and unsubscribe

If you receive a newsletter or marketing email from us, you can stop at any time:

  • Click the unsubscribe link included at the bottom of every newsletter and marketing email, or
  • Email cooper@agentsetup.io with the word "Unsubscribe" in the subject line from the address you want removed.

We process unsubscribe requests within 10 business days. Unsubscribing from newsletters does not stop service emails about an order you placed, such as scheduling or refund messages. If Digistore24 sends you marketing emails, you can also opt out through the unsubscribe link in those emails.

Who we share information with

  • Digistore24, which processes payments as our reseller under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers we use to run the business, such as email and file storage, only as needed to deliver your service.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep order and communication records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax record-keeping requirements. Tool access you grant for a build is removed when the engagement ends, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses. To make a request, email cooper@agentsetup.io. We respond within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
